Clipping Engine

Clipping Engine is a private, internal tool of a small short-video clipping studio. It turns long videos and live-stream recordings into short vertical clips with subtitles.

How it uses Google Drive

The tool signs in to one Google account owned by the studio and asks only for the drive.file permission. With it, the tool can only see and manage the files and folders it creates itself (the finished clips and their transcripts). It cannot read any other file in the account's Google Drive.

The tool is not offered to the public and does not access any other person's Google account.
